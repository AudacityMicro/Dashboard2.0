Dashboard 2.0

Privacy policy

Effective date: November 8, 2026. Maintainer: AudacityMicro. Contact: aj@audacitymicro.com.

What Dashboard handles

The capture inbox stores the text you save, voice recordings you start, images/audio you select or share, file types, timestamps, capture identifiers and sync status. The dashboard stores tasks, completion choices and cached agent updates. Recordings require microphone permission and stop when the capture screen leaves the foreground.

Local storage

Capture payloads, the capture database and dashboard task state are kept in app-private storage excluded from Android backup. Android device security protects this storage; the app does not add separate per-note encryption. These local records remain until app data is cleared or the app is uninstalled. The preview has no individual capture-deletion control. Unsynced data is lost if app data is cleared, the app is uninstalled, or the device is lost.

Optional Google account and Drive access

If you connect Google Drive, Dashboard requests drive.file, openid and email permissions. The app uses Google's stable account identifier to bind an installation and its uploads to the selected account. It does not need access to unrelated Drive files. The first selected account remains bound to that installation.

Google Play Services manages reusable authorization credentials in its protected account storage. Dashboard holds short-lived access tokens in memory and does not save them to app files, preferences, databases, logs or Android backup. The app saves the account identifier and sync-enabled state locally.

How synchronization uses your data

When enabled, synchronization sends captures and associated metadata to your own Google Drive. It also creates a home-board file, a phone task-state file and an agent instructions file. The phone reads the agent board, writes phone task changes and preserves later edits to the instructions file. Drive files remain under your Google account's controls and retention rules.

The Dashboard capture/Drive component sends this information to Google for the requested synchronization. It does not send captures or Google account data to a maintainer-operated capture server, advertising system or AI model. Dashboard's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

Your separate AI agent

Dashboard does not automatically grant another agent access. If you connect an agent through its own tools, that agent and its providers may read or process the Drive files you authorize. Agent instructions may ask the agent to maintain its own guidance and memory and connect email/calendar separately. Those connections, schedules and processing are performed by the agent under the permissions you grant it, not through Dashboard's Drive token. Review that provider's practices before connecting it.

Other launcher features and network requests

Dashboard retains Kvaesitso launcher features. Depending on enabled features and permissions, the launcher may access installed apps, contacts, calendar data, local files, location and configured external services to provide search, widgets or related functions. Review feature settings and Android permissions. Future preview builds with manual update checks contact GitHub only when you request a check; GitHub receives ordinary network information such as the device's IP address. Browser downloads and Google services are also subject to their providers' policies.

Your controls and deletion

You can decline Drive sign-in, deny optional Android permissions, or pause synchronization. Pausing stops new sync work but an in-flight request may finish, and it does not revoke Google's grant. Revoke access through your Google Account's third-party connections settings. Remove uploaded files through Google Drive and its trash controls. Revoking access does not delete local or Drive copies; deleting Drive files does not remove the local original. Clear Android app storage or uninstall to remove local records; this also erases unsynced content. Contact your agent provider separately to remove copies held by that provider.

Support and changes

If you contact support, the maintainer receives what you choose to send and uses it to respond. Do not send passwords, authorization tokens or private captures unless needed for your request. Changes to data practices will be reflected in an updated policy date and relevant in-app disclosures. Contact the maintainer with privacy or deletion questions.
