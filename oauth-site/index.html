Dashboard 2.0

Capture a thought. Keep your next steps close.

Dashboard 2.0 is an Android home-screen launcher based on Kvaesitso. Search and launch your apps, save text notes, record voice notes, select images, and manage a to-do list.

Your inbox, available offline

Captures and task changes are saved on your phone first. When you connect Google Drive, Dashboard queues uploads to your own Drive and refreshes your agent updates and tasks. Your agent connects to Drive separately through its own tools.

Optional Google Drive connection

Dashboard requests access to the specific Drive files used with this app and basic sign-in information to keep your captures attached to the correct account. It does not request access to your entire Drive, Gmail, or Google Calendar through this connection.

Dashboard is currently a preview. Google sign-in is limited to configured test users while production setup is completed. Offline capture works without Google sign-in.
